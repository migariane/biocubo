## Sobre nosotros

Welcome to **BIO³** (BIOCUBO), a research group at the [University of Granada](https://www.ugr.es) where mathematicians, statisticians, and bioinformaticians collaborate on real problems in biosciences, epidemiology, and public health. We believe rigorous quantitative methods are essential for understanding biological systems and improving health outcomes.

El nombre **BIO³** refleja la intersección tridimensional de tres disciplinas cuantitativas:

- **Bioinformática**
- **Bioestadística**  
- **Biomatemáticas**

---

## What We Do

We work across several interconnected research areas:

- **Causal Inference** — understanding cause-and-effect relationships in observational data
- **Mathematical Modeling** — capturing disease dynamics and population processes
- **Computational Statistics** — Monte Carlo methods, Bayesian inference, modern machine learning
- **Bioinformatics & Omics** — genomic, transcriptomic, and proteomic data analysis
- **Quantitative Epidemiology** — pharmacoepidemiology, observational studies, real-world evidence
- **Clinical Biostatistics** — clinical trials, longitudinal data, survival analysis
- **Biomathematics** — transport equations, fluid mechanics, stochastic modeling, gene regulation

---

## Our Mission

We're guided by three core commitments:

### Research Excellence
Push the boundaries of quantitative methods applied to real-world biosciences problems.

### Knowledge Sharing
Host quarterly seminars open to the scientific community—we learn by teaching others.

### Collaboration
Build bridges across disciplines and institutions. Good science happens at intersections.
