**Scope & visitor mode.** Read-first website (consulta) with a persuasive portada. Pages: `index.qmd`, `members.qmd`, `seminars.qmd`, `about.qmd`.

**Audience / job / action.** Pares científicos y colaboradores (UGR y externos): entender qué es BIO³, quién es quién, qué líneas hay y cuándo es el próximo seminario. Proof: bios reales, ciclo trimestral con resúmenes, afiliación UGR. Primary action: abrir el próximo seminario / contactar coordinadores.

**Constraints (pinned).** BIO³ cube logo + UGR crest always visible; typography Newsreader + Inter only (translation of the world's hand-lettering: Newsreader *italic* carries the handwritten voice, Inter 800 uppercase tracked carries the block-caps voice); every content fact intact; Spanish copy.

**Chosen direction.** *Atlas de Retallos* (`memory-quilt-atlas`), bolder hand round 1, seed key `799d0885`, winner on both axes against the dealt leader. Memorable moment: the portada as a pieced quilt whose six patches are the six disciplines, sewn with red running stitch, the group statement on a linen label pinned to it.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The group site is a provenance atlas of cloth — every member, seminar and discipline is a patch with body, date and history. It refuses the navy-university hero and the grid of identical cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Linen `#F2ECE1` ground; chambray `#A7B8C7`, indigo `#495F74`, funeral black `#1B1B1D`, pinstripe `#E7E3D6` as full-field cloths; stitch red `#D62B28` as the only accent, always as running-stitch dashed rules and buttons; iron-gall `#7A5C3A` captions. Unequal patch grid, linen labels, red dashed stitch borders, cloth swatch chips.

**STORY:** Visitor lands on a quilt, reads the label, sees the disciplines pieced together, finds a person or a date, leaves knowing this group stitches methods to people.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Linen label panel left (BIO³ logo, name in Newsreader italic, subtitle, UGR crest patch); quilt field right — six unequal discipline patches in different cloths, red running stitch between every seam; the stitches draw as it enters.

**FORM:** Atlas de Retallos (challenger, rank 1 of the bolder hand), seed key `799d0885`, register bolder, code-led.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

**Unresolved:** whether past-seminar photos read better sewn (ditched frame) or taped; how large the fundador patch spans on mid widths.
